# Research Questions

The market census and canonical research must answer these questions rather than assuming the answers.

1. How often do Malaysia FinOps Engineer vacancies require prior cloud / engineering experience?
2. How often do they require Finance or commercial knowledge?
3. How often do they explicitly mention architecture, TCO, or design review?
4. How often do they mention policy-as-code, IaC, CI/CD, or guardrails?
5. How often do they require anomaly detection and automation?
6. How often do they mention reservations / commitments as a recurring responsibility?
7. How often do they mention showback / chargeback?
8. How often do they mention contract, licensing, or vendor management?
9. How often do they require communication with Engineering, Finance, Procurement, or business owners?
10. How often do they require reporting tools plus technical cloud knowledge?
11. Is FinOps in Malaysia primarily positioned as a dedicated role or embedded cloud discipline?
12. What evidence exists for shift-left FinOps in official case studies?
13. Which real cases show automated resource-lifecycle control?
14. Which real cases show architecture substitution rather than simple rightsizing?
15. Which real cases validate savings after implementation instead of reporting recommendation-engine estimates?
